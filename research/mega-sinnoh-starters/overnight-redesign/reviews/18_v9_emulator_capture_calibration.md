# v9 Emulator Capture Calibration

The initial 45-input trial overshot the sprite reveal and reached Rowan's later advice dialogue. A press-by-press 0–45 contact sheet localized the sprite-loading transition to press 13/14. Full-size inspection of press 13 shows the top screen still darkened during the transition, with Rowan visible but the Mega sprite not yet fully rendered. Therefore the production capture should issue exactly 13 A presses, then wait for the reveal animation to settle without sending a fourteenth input. The prior contact sheet is not accepted as v9 evidence; fresh captures must come from the six v9 resource-specific ROM variants and retain a clean native DS framebuffer crop.

A second timing experiment at normal speed was invalid because the same input count was consumed at the title/copyright sequence; all final capture runs must therefore retain `--disable-limiter` or use state-based synchronization. Full-size inspection of the earlier accelerated run at press 20 shows Rowan alone with the dialogue “However, everyone just calls me the Pokémon Professor.” The sprite is not merely lost in the contact-sheet thumbnail. This means the exact reveal point remains later in the intro, or the prior v6 evidence was captured from a different state/harness. The next step is to identify the Buneary show/hide state transition in `rowan_intro_app.c` and synchronize capture to it rather than continue guessing input counts.
