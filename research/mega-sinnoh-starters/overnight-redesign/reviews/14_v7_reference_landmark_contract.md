This contract records the visual direction recovered from the primary Rocketmanga concept board and the approved Monkey King Infernape reference. It supersedes the simplified geometric interpretation used in v6.

## DS translation rules

At 80×80, the references must be translated rather than traced. The species silhouette should occupy roughly 60–72 pixels in its largest dimension while retaining at least a two-pixel safety margin. Organic forms must use irregular stepped clusters, not long clean polygons. Primary landmarks receive the strongest contrast; secondary internal markings must not fragment the silhouette. Rear views must be reconstructed from anatomy, not produced by deleting the face from the front.

The two frames must visibly change at least two landmark regions—such as canopy tips plus head shift, staff angle plus flame tips, or wing-tip/mantle motion plus crown plume—without destabilizing anatomy. A candidate is not eligible for production merely because it passes file-format and palette limits.

## Pixel-reference construction lessons

The discovered Torterra set demonstrates that a strong Mega-like redraw can remain compact while using a continuous, organic silhouette. Its shell mass curves around the neck and flank rather than becoming a rectangular platform. The canopy uses many short, staggered leaf clusters; the trunk and shell plates overlap with visible depth; the front and rear views change which spikes, legs, and trunk planes are visible. V7 may borrow these structural lessons, but must keep the project’s approved fortress-tree and armored-face identity rather than copying that set’s exact design.

The discovered Infernape front/back reference demonstrates the correct density for Gen IV sprite anatomy: compact crouched limbs, alternating one- to three-pixel outline steps, small highlight islands embedded inside larger color masses, and a rear view rebuilt around shoulder, cape, hip, and tail overlap. Its mane is swept and directional rather than circular. V7 should combine that density and anatomical compression with the approved two-handed flame-staff concept; it must not reproduce the reference’s exact pose, costume, or flame shapes.

The discovered Empoleon set provides a useful rear-view density model: the crown has distinct back planes, the shoulder mantle overlaps the torso, the rear flippers separate from the cloak, and small warm accents keep the back from collapsing into one dark mass. Its front pose also shows how one arm can carry a large shield-like form while the opposite flipper extends, creating hierarchy instead of perfect bilateral symmetry. V7 should use those construction principles while retaining VilliamBoom’s gold-edged emperor blades rather than copying the reference sprite’s shield device.

The full-size VilliamBoom sheet clarifies the intended redesigns. Torterra is long and low, with a green armored face under multiple lateral horns, huge white shell spikes, and a broad forest canopy; the fortress feeling comes from spike architecture and armored shell layering, not literal rectangular masonry. Infernape’s strongest identity is a compact crouch, white directional mane, gold scroll armor, blue hands and feet, and a flame-backed circular staff/ring motif; the project-specific approved adaptation remains a single two-handed staff. Empoleon is a narrow dark emperor with an exaggerated gold crown mask, one enormous foreground blade-wing, a secondary swept mantle wing, a pale beak/face plume, and gold talons. These asymmetrical depth relationships must drive v7.
