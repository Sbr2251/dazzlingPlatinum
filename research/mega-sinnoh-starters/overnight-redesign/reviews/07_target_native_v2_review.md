# Target-Native v2 Ruthless Review

## Decision summary

**No species is promoted wholesale from v2.** The pass is treated as a controlled experiment, and only pixel-level changes that genuinely improve the sprite will survive into v3.

| Species | Front verdict | Rear verdict | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Torterra | Strong transformed fortress silhouette; face, eye, jaw, plate facets, and four heavy legs read more clearly than v1. The canopy, trunk, shell, head, and legs remain one coherent organism. | True rear view with excellent shell mass and a visibly embedded trunk. Body and leg scale match the front. | **Retain v2 as the v3 base.** |
| Infernape | Reject. The broad torso repaint became a large flat brown polygon that erased arm, chest, and hip articulation. The extra blue grip pixels read like repeated square markers instead of two anatomical hands. | Reject for the same reason. The staff remains continuous, but the body is flatter and less simian than v1. | **Revert to v1**, then make only surgical hand/forearm edits. |
| Empoleon | Broad emperor silhouette remains successful, but the new pale shoulder clasps are too rectangular and risk reading as pasted-on pieces. Crown pieces are clearer in places but not yet uniformly joined. | The rear remains coherent and correctly oriented, but the crown/shoulder repairs are visually busier without enough gain over v1. | **Revert to v1**, then retain only a minimal crown-root and shoulder-attachment repair. |

## Non-negotiable v3 corrections

Torterra must preserve v2’s face and trunk-shell continuity. Infernape must preserve v1’s articulated body and replace only the exact grip/forearm clusters needed to prove that both hands hold one staff. Empoleon must preserve v1’s broad torso and wing planes while adding no more than a few connecting pixels at each shoulder and the crown root. Any large polygon repaint is prohibited because it destroys the hand-clustered Nintendo DS look.
