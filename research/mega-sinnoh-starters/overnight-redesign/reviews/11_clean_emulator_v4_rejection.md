# Clean Emulator Review — v4 Rejection

## Decision

**Reject v4 as final despite successful native-format validation and unobscured rendering.** The new sprites are unmistakably different from the base starters and have stronger Mega silhouettes, but they do not yet meet the required standard for polished Pokémon Platinum battle sprites.

## Cross-species findings

The clean DeSmuME contact sheet confirms that the harness now exposes every sprite without a dialogue box. It also reveals that the generated-concept reduction retained too much irregular pseudo-pixel texture. Compared with Rowan’s authentic sprite, the candidates use noisier edge clusters, weaker face landmarks, heavier near-black masses, and less controlled highlight ramps. Technical validity is therefore not visual acceptance.

## Mega Infernape front — hard rejection

At native 256×192 emulator resolution, Infernape’s face is largely lost inside an oversized pale mane. The torso and near arm collapse into a dark vertical mass. The staff is not read immediately as one deliberate Monkey King weapon held by two hands; its diagonal shaft and flame endpoints compete with body contours. The stance is too compressed and hunched, and the feet are tiny relative to the upper mass. Although the silhouette is transformed, the result resembles a reduced concept illustration more than a hand-authored Platinum sprite.

Required next changes:

1. Reduce and reshape the pale mane to expose a clear red/brown face mask and ear/crest landmarks.
2. Establish a readable chest plane with separate warm armor and dark shadow clusters.
3. Draw one continuous staff with a simple three-tone ramp and unmistakable hand-over-shaft pixels at two grip points.
4. Widen the lower stance and strengthen both feet.
5. Remove incidental dark texture and use larger intentional clusters.
6. Rebalance the palette so the canonical Infernape cream, warm red-orange, and deep brown remain identifiable instead of merging into black-gold noise.

## Next loop

The next pass must be a native-resolution structural repaint rather than another automatic reduction. It should begin from simplified silhouette masks, manually define face/weapon/anatomy landmarks, and then apply controlled 15-color ramps. Torterra and Empoleon also require individual native-screen reviews before any acceptance decision.

## Mega Torterra front — hard rejection

The fortress/tree concept is evident, but the native-screen sprite reads first as a low stump with a canopy rather than as a powerful Torterra. The face and forward body are buried beneath the pale shell/ramp structure, the legs are thin repetitive columns, and the underside is a noisy picket-fence texture. The canopy and left-side foliage carry more visual weight than the head. Brown, green, cream, and near-black pixels are distributed in many small clusters rather than the larger controlled masses typical of Platinum.

Required next changes:

1. Enlarge and expose the head, with an unmistakable jaw, eye, horn, and forward-facing facial plane.
2. Build four legs as distinct weight-bearing anatomical forms rather than repeated vertical strips.
3. Simplify the underside into two or three large shadow clusters.
4. Integrate the fortress shell as a readable armored dome/ridge behind the head rather than a pale horizontal slab.
5. Reduce canopy noise and retain the tree as a secondary silhouette anchor.
6. Restore a clearer Torterra green/brown/stone palette hierarchy with fewer near-black edge fragments.

## Mega Empoleon front — hard rejection

Empoleon has a transformed broad emperor silhouette, but the native-screen result is too amorphous and dark. The head, beak, chest, and crown merge into the top edge; the blade wings look like detached black wedges; the torso is a rounded blue blob without a strong penguin breast or shoulder structure; and the tiny feet do not support the mass. Gold pixels are scattered as fine spikes instead of forming an intentional crown and trim. The broad cape idea is useful, but the execution is not yet a polished Platinum sprite.

Required next changes:

1. Define a clear head and pale beak/face wedge above a distinct chest plane.
2. Connect each wing blade to a visible shoulder root and reduce the pure-black triangular mass.
3. Replace scattered gold spikes with a compact integrated trident crown and a small number of deliberate armor accents.
4. Reshape the body into a recognizable upright penguin/emperor silhouette rather than a low rounded cape.
5. Enlarge and separate the feet.
6. Use broader navy, cobalt, pale-blue, and gold clusters with cleaner ramps and less jagged texture.

## Overall v4 conclusion

All three sprites pass the transformation gate but fail the **Platinum-authenticity, anatomy, landmark readability, and cluster-discipline gates**. They remain valuable silhouette references only. The next candidate must be reconstructed as native 80×80 pixel art from simplified masks and manually placed landmarks, not produced by further automatic shrinking or palette quantization of the generated maquettes.
