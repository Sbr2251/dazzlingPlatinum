# Target-native v5 ruthless review

## Decision

**Reject v5 as a final set.** It is a major structural improvement over v4 and finally reads as three deliberate Mega transformations, but it does not yet pass the attachment and authentic-cluster gates.

## Torterra

The front now has a readable horned animal head, three planted legs, a separate dark body, an armored fortress shell, and a secondary tree. The rear is truly rear-facing and no longer displays a frontal head. This is the first candidate whose concept is immediately “Mega Torterra fortress” rather than a base Torterra trace or a stump.

Remaining failures: the shell is still too rectangular and orthographic, the pale battlement face forms one large flat panel, the canopy is an overly even horizontal cloud, and the front head/shell relationship is visually crowded. v6 must break the shell into stepped planes, reduce the canopy’s rectangular highlight, and add diagonal cluster transitions without losing the strong body landmarks.

## Infernape

The larger mane, crest, armor, thick legs, and flame-ended staff create unmistakable Monkey King Mega identity. Front and rear orientation are distinguishable, the face survives at native scale, and the palette is substantially more readable than v4.

**Critical rejection:** the staff is not actually held. In front, the diagonal shaft passes behind the body but misses both painted grips; the hands read as empty fists. The rear has the same failure with an opposite diagonal. This violates the explicit non-negotiable staff-to-two-hands continuity gate. The torso and legs are also too vertically rectangular and humanlike. v6 must route one continuous shaft through two clearly wrapped grips in both orientations, reconnect arms to those grips, shorten the torso, and bend the legs into a more simian fighting stance.

## Empoleon

The front has a broad emperor silhouette, integrated short crown, visible head and beak, shoulder-rooted blade wings, mantle, breast plane, and large supporting feet. The rear removes the white breast and substitutes a broad navy back with gold insignia, so it is now genuinely rear-facing. Both are immediately recognizable as Mega Empoleon and no longer resemble the base sprite.

Remaining failures: the bilateral symmetry is too perfect, the blade wings read as flat gold panels, and the front breast is a single overlarge pale wedge. v6 must introduce shoulder depth, stagger the wing tips, reduce the white breast area, and add controlled navy/cobalt plane breaks while preserving the broad emperor mass.

## v6 acceptance targets

1. Infernape staff intersects two visible wraparound grips in every frame and view.
2. No grip, flame, crown, wing, tree, battlement, or foot exists as an orphan component.
3. Torterra shell and Empoleon torso use stepped, asymmetric cluster planes rather than large geometric slabs.
4. All views remain within 80×80, use no more than 15 visible colors, and remain recognizable at native size without labels.
5. Animation changes internal clusters without translating or detaching whole anatomy.
