# Arc 2, part 1: Jubilife to the Resonator (draft)

Claude's draft, mirrored in the "Arc 2 Screenplay" tab of the story doc. Darren (the player, girl or boy) is a silent
protagonist apart from Yes/No choices. Lines marked Note, and bracketed asides, are for us, not in-game text.

## Scene 2.1: Jubilife City, finding Cyrus

- *Arc 2 opens right after Gym 1. Darren and Garius come back to Jubilife, as Rowan asked. The plaza is empty, and the big screen is dark. Cyrus stands in front of it, alone.*
- **Garius:** "There he is. Spacesuit guy."
- **Cyrus:** "...Rowan sent you."
- **Cyrus:** "The rift opened again. I felt it."
- **Cyrus:** "Then bring him here. Everyone. There is something you should all hear before I take another step with you."
- > Note: "I felt it" is a small plant: Giratina left him attuned to its world.

## Scene 2.2: Trainers' School, after hours: the confession

- *An empty classroom in the evening. Rowan, Ruth, Garius and Darren sit at the students' desks. Cyrus stands at the front, where the teacher would.*
- **Rowan:** "Ruth's readings put the Oreburgh rift on Route 204 now, inside Ravaged Path. I'd like you to guide these two through it."
- **Ruth:** "The totem's in there too. The readings are... loud."
- **Cyrus:** "I will. But not as a stranger."
- **Cyrus:** "My name is Cyrus. In the world I came from, I led an organization called Team Galactic."
- **Cyrus:** "I believed spirit was a flaw. That grief and anger and hope were the reasons the world was broken. So I set out to make a new world, without them."
- **Cyrus:** "To do it, I chained the guardians of the lakes. I tore at them until they gave me what I needed to summon Dialga and Palkia."
- **Ruth:** "...The lake guardians? Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf?"
- **Cyrus:** "A boy stopped me. A boy, and a Champion. And something older than all of us dragged me into its world for what I'd done."
- **Cyrus:** "I don't know how long I was there. Long enough to stop counting."
- **Rowan:** "...Years. In that place. And you walked out alive."
- > Note: Rowan's track: the first crack in "too good to be true." Someone came back from Giratina's world.
- *Garius stands up so fast his desk scrapes the floor.*
- **Garius:** "You're kidding me. You CHAINED UP Pokemon? Legendary Pokemon? And you want us to follow you back in there?!"
- **Garius:** "Some of us have actually lost-"
- *He stops himself.*
- **Garius:** "...Forget it."
- > Note: Hint G3 about his father: he starts the sentence and cuts himself off.
- **Rowan:** "Sit down, Garius."
- **Garius:** "No! Professor, you heard him! He should go. Back wherever he came from."
- **Cyrus:** "He's right to be angry. I won't ask anyone to forgive me."
- **Rowan:** "I'm not asking you to ask."
- **Rowan:** "You came out of that portal and walked straight back in, for two children you'd never met. What you were is not what you are."
- *Rowan sets his briefcase on the teacher's desk and opens it. One Poke Ball is left inside.*
- **Rowan:** "One starter is left. It came out of that world in this case. In a way, so did you."
- **Rowan:** "This can be a new start for you."
- *Cyrus looks at the Poke Ball for a long moment before he takes it. [He gets the remaining starter: the one Darren's beats, and that beats Garius's.]*
- **Cyrus:** "...I have not held one of these since I lost everything."
- **Garius:** "Unbelievable. Count me out. I'm not following him anywhere."
- *Garius storms out.*
- **Ruth:** "Garius, wait-!"
- **Rowan:** "Let him go. He'll cool off."
- > Note: Garius heard the Ravaged Path plan before he left. That matters for the first leak.
- > Note: His anger here is genuine: Eclipse hasn't recruited him yet. That keeps this scene honest when players look back after the reveal.
- > Note: "Since I lost everything" explains why Cyrus has no Pokemon: none came through the rift with him.

## Scene 2.3: Outside the Trainers' School, first battle with Cyrus

- **Cyrus:** "It has been a long time since I battled beside a Pokemon that chose to stand with me."
- **Cyrus:** "Show me how it's done."
- *Battle: Cyrus, with his one starter. Losing doesn't end the game.*
- **Cyrus** (after the battle) "Hm. It listened to me. I didn't expect that."
- **Cyrus:** "Ravaged Path is north, on Route 204. I'll meet you at the rift."
- **Rowan:** "Ruth will go with you and watch the readings from outside. Be careful, all of you."
- > Note: Battle 1 of 5 with Cyrus: curious, almost gentle.

## Scene 2.4: Cutaway: Team Eclipse

- *Darren isn't in this scene. A dark room lit by a violet glow. Kahn and Indra stand over a table. An Eclipse Shard pulses on it.*
- **Kahn:** "Word from our new friend. The professor has a guide now."
- **Kahn:** "A man who walked out of the Distortion World. Alive."
- **Indra:** "...Out of it."
- **Indra:** "Then the door opens both ways."
- **Kahn:** "They're going into the Ravaged Path rift tomorrow. That site is ours."
- **Indra:** "Then be there first."
- > Note: Never show who sent the message. Fair-play clue: the informant describes the guide from the outside, as "a man who walked out." Cyrus wouldn't talk about himself that way.
- > Note: Indra's "the door opens both ways" plants her whole arc: if someone can come out of that world, her brother can too.
- > Note: Garius's recruitment itself happens off screen, to protect the twist. In hindsight: he pocketed an Eclipse flyer at the Act 1 rally.

## Scene 2.5: Ravaged Path, Eclipse is waiting (the first leak)

- *Route 204, outside Ravaged Path. Ruth sets up her readings at the entrance. Cyrus is waiting. Inside, by the back wall, two Eclipse grunts stand beside a violet rift.*
- **Eclipse Grunt:** "Right on time. Just like we were told."
- **Eclipse Grunt** (looking at Cyrus) "So you're the one who walked out. Huh. Thought you'd be taller."
- *Battle: a double battle, Darren and Cyrus against the two grunts.*
- **Eclipse Grunt:** "Doesn't matter. This site's given us all it's going to."
- *The grunts run out the north exit, toward Floaroma.*
- *Bolted to the cave wall beside the rift: a cracked metal frame, scorched violet. Restraints hang from it, empty.*
- **Cyrus:** "..."
- **Cyrus:** "I have seen a machine like this before."
- *He doesn't say where. [Plant: the player knows it's the kind of thing he built for the lake guardians.]*
- **Ruth** (running in from the entrance) "How did they know we'd be here? Only the five of us knew."
- **Cyrus:** "...Yes. I know how that looks."
- > Note: First leak (L0). On the surface it points at Cyrus: he just confessed, and the grunts recognize him. In hindsight: Garius heard the plan, and the grunts know Cyrus only as "the one who walked out," the way someone else described him.

## Scene 2.6: Through the rift: Distortion World puzzle

- *Darren and Cyrus step through. Ruth stays outside with the readings.*
- *[PUZZLE: the Ravaged Path rift. NEEDS DESIGN. One new twist on the Act 1 wall-walk.]*
- **Cyrus:** "It's frightened. The whole space is. Can you feel how it shakes?"
- **Cyrus:** "Follow the seam. ...Not that one. That one is a scar."
- *Halfway through, a huge shadow moves far below the platforms, then it's gone. Cyrus doesn't look down. [Plant: Giratina watching. Only the player can notice.]*

## Scene 2.7: The Hitmonlee totem

- *A platform at the far end. A Hitmonlee, far too big, wreathed in a violet aura, with a shard lodged in its chest. It screams when it sees them.*
- *Totem battle (the existing Hitmonlee totem encounter: aura and ally summons).*
- *After the battle: the aura drains away. The Hitmonlee shrinks back to its normal size, shaking. The shard falls from its chest, and as the violet fades, it turns clear.*
- **Cyrus:** "...It's calm."
- *The Hitmonlee nudges the clear shard toward Darren. It bows, then bounds off into the dark. [Calmed totems go home. They can be caught in the postgame.]*
- *Darren got a Calm Shard!*
- **Cyrus** (looking at the shard) "I took. You were given."
- **Cyrus:** "Come. The rift will close behind us."

## Scene 2.8: Route 204: the Resonator, and Garius comes back

- *Back outside, the rift snaps shut behind them. Ruth runs over.*
- **Ruth:** "The readings just dropped to nothing! You did it! ...Wait. What is THAT?"
- *Darren shows Ruth the Calm Shard.*
- **Ruth:** "It's clear. The Professor's shard is violet, and it pulses. Like it hurts. This one's just... warm."
- **Ruth:** "Give me one night. I've got an idea."
- *Fade. Morning, at the north end of Route 204. Ruth hands Darren a device.*
- **Ruth:** "I call it the Resonator! It holds your Calm Shard, and it lets you call on that Hitmonlee's strength. Try it on that rock!"
- *A cracked boulder blocks the path. Using the Resonator: "Hitmonlee's energy surges through the Resonator!" A ghostly Hitmonlee kicks the rock apart. [Field power: needs the Coal Badge plus the calmed Hitmonlee totem.]*
- **Ruth:** "It works! ...Roark said that badge means Oreburgh vouches for you. I think the totem knew that."
- *Garius walks up from the north, from the direction of Floaroma.*
- **Garius:** "Hey. I went to cool off in the flower fields. ...So. You beat the giant Hitmonlee?"
- **Garius:** "Fine. Good. Whatever. I'm heading to Eterna for my next badge. Don't bring him."
- *Garius leaves to the north.*
- **Cyrus** (quietly) "He's not wrong to keep his distance."
- > Note: Clue in hindsight: the grunts fled north toward Floaroma, and Garius walks in from Floaroma soon after.
- > Note: Ruth's line explains the badge half of the field-power rule for the player.
