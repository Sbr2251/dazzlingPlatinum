# Secrets ledger

Each secret tracks when the **player** learns it versus when each **character** learns it. The gap between
the two is the dramatic irony. Each beat gets one of three tags:

- **plant**: the player sees it, and the characters don't notice it.
- **twist the knife**: a character walks right past the truth.
- **payoff**: the character finds out.

Rule: every plant needs a payoff. "?" means not decided yet. Facts live in `bible.md`.

## Track L: the leak (a mystery, not irony)

Here the player is kept in the dark on purpose. The player has known since Act 1 that Cyrus led Team Galactic,
so they'll suspect him first. Their own knowledge of Platinum becomes the misdirection.

Rules for a fair twist:

- Every leak must be explainable by **either** Cyrus or Garius: both were present, or both could have known.
- Each leak should look more like Cyrus on the surface (he goes off alone, he's cryptic, he knows Eclipse's
  methods), while a careful player can notice Garius was always there too.
- Garius reveals himself ("Darren, let's not play any more games. It was me all along."), right after Darren and
  Rowan start trusting Cyrus again. Nobody catches him, so the clues have to be there in hindsight.
- Leader scenes can show Eclipse receiving a tip, never who sent it.
- Garius's Act 1 line ("Team Eclipse really is something") reads differently after the reveal.
