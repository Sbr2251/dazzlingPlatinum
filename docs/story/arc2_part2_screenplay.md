# Arc 2, part 2: Floaroma to Celestic (draft)

Claude's draft, mirrored in the "Arc 2 Screenplay" tab of the story doc. Continues from part 1 (Route 204, the Resonator).

## Scene 2.9: Floaroma Town and Valley Windworks (leak L1)

- *Floaroma Town, evening. Ruth checks her readings outside the Pokemon Center. Garius is there, buying honey at the flower shop.*
- **Ruth:** "There's something at Valley Windworks, east of here. Faint, but it reads like Ravaged Path did. We'll check it first thing in the morning."
- **Garius:** "Whatever. I'm going through the forest tonight."
- *Garius leaves toward Eterna Forest.*
- **Cyrus:** "I'll scout the road to the Windworks tonight. I don't sleep much anymore."
- *Cyrus walks off into the dark alone. Ruth watches him go.*
- *Morning, Valley Windworks. The building is empty. Bolt holes in the floor, drag marks, a generator still warm to the touch.*
- **Windworks Worker:** "Those folks in the dark coats? Cleared out about an hour ago. Got a call, packed up in a real hurry."
- **Ruth** (quietly) "A call."
- *Ruth looks at Cyrus.*
- **Cyrus:** "I saw lights here last night. By the time I reached the fence, they were loading trucks. I came back to tell you."
- **Ruth:** "...You could have woken us."
- > Note: Leak L1. On the surface it points at Cyrus: he went out alone at night, and he was here. In hindsight: Garius heard the plan too, and left that night.

## Scene 2.12: The Eclipse Haven (side plot)

- **Gardenia:** "Can I ask you something? Off the record?"
- **Gardenia:** "There's a shelter in town, the Eclipse Haven. They take in Pokemon whose trainers have passed on. I brought them three Pokemon last spring."
- **Gardenia:** "Not one has been adopted. And now they won't let me visit. Something's wrong. I can feel it, the way you can tell a plant isn't getting any sun."
- *The Haven: the old Galactic building, now hung with an Eclipse banner: A HOME FOR THE ONES LEFT BEHIND. Gardenia and Ruth come along. Cyrus waits outside.*
- **Haven Receptionist:** "No visitors today, I'm afraid. The Pokemon are resting."
- **Gardenia:** "They're always resting."
- *A janitor mopping the lobby floor edges over to Darren.*
- **Janitor:** "Psst. You, with the Pokedex. Do not look at me. Look at the floor. It is very clean."
- **Janitor:** "I am Looker. International Police. ...You did not hear that."
- **Looker:** "There is a service door around the back. In five minutes it will be unlocked. I will be... mopping elsewhere."
- *The back corridor: rows of kennels, every one empty, each with a name tag. Gardenia stops at three of them.*
- **Gardenia:** "Bramble... Pip... Clover. These are mine. Where are they?"
- *A cramped office. Looker is going through a filing cabinet.*
- **Looker:** "Crates. Every week, labeled "supplies." Look where they go."
- *The shipping manifests list destinations: Ravaged Path. Eterna Forest. Route 214.*
- **Ruth:** "Those are... those are my rift readings. Every single one of them."
- **Looker:** "Pokemon go in. Crates go out. And wherever the crates go, a rift tears open."
- *Two Haven caretakers in aprons block the door: Eclipse grunts.*
- **Haven Caretaker:** "Staff only, sweetie. You're going to have to leave. Now."
- *Double battle: Darren and Gardenia against the two caretakers.*
- *In the loading bay: a handful of Pokemon in crates, ready to ship. One of them is a Budew.*
- **Gardenia:** "Pip! Oh, Pip..."
- **Gardenia:** "...Bramble and Clover aren't here. They already sent them."
- *Looker calls it in. The Haven is shut down.*
- **Looker:** "This is bigger than one building. Whatever they do at those sites, I intend to see it with my own eyes."
- **Looker:** "One more thing. The man who travels with you, in the... unusual suit. What is his name?"
- **Ruth:** "Cyrus."
- **Looker:** "Cyrus. Hm."
- *He writes it down.*
- **Looker:** "If you see me again, you have not seen me."
- > Note: The characters now know Pokemon are being sent to the rift sites, but not what's done to them. The player can already guess. Looker's proof comes in Pastoria.
- > Note: Fair-play clue: Garius didn't know about the Haven (he'd already left town), and this is the one raid Eclipse didn't see coming.
- > Note: "What is his name?" sets up Veilstone: "This man does not exist."

## Scene 2.14: Mt. Coronet, Route 211

- *Rowan has asked everyone to meet him in Celestic Town, so Garius comes too, keeping his distance from Cyrus. Inside Mt. Coronet, the air gets warmer. A grate in the floor glows a dull orange from somewhere far below.*
- **Ruth:** "It's hot. Mountains aren't supposed to be hot on the inside."
- **Garius:** "Great. Even the mountain's broken."
- *Cyrus stops. He puts a hand flat on the rock wall and stays there, very still.*
- **Ruth:** "Cyrus?"
- **Cyrus:** "...It's nothing. Keep moving."
- *For a moment, deep in the glow under the grate, something shimmers violet-black. Then it's gone. [Plant: the molten depths and the Umbral Palace, where the race for Giratina happens in Arc 3. Only the player sees the shimmer.]*

## Scene 2.15: Celestic Town, the elder's warning

- *The shrine in Celestic. Rowan is waiting with the town elder, Cynthia's grandmother. A tall man in a long coat stands nearby with his hands behind his back: Saros. The townspeople give him a wide berth.*
- **Rowan:** "Thank you for seeing us, elder. These are the ones I told you about."
- **Elder:** "The children who went into the torn world. And came back."
- **Elder:** "Sit. Old women tell stories. Sometimes the stories are true."
- **Elder:** "The three who made this world, time and space and the world beneath, were never meant to be held. Not by any hand."
- **Elder:** "Every age, someone tries. Always for a reason that sounds good. A war to end. A love to save. A grief to undo."
- **Elder:** "And every time, they bend time and space to fix their own sorrow, and leave the whole world with more of it."
- **Elder:** "There is one more story. When the three are threatened, the renegade one, the one who lives beneath, sends someone back through the torn world. A guide. Someone who has seen its home."
- *Rowan's eyes go to Cyrus. Cyrus is staring at the shrine.*
- **Saros:** "With respect, elder, that's what people say once they've made their peace with losing. Some of us haven't."
- **Garius:** "Yeah. Old stories. It's easy to tell people to let go when you've never lost anything."
- **Elder** (to Garius) "Child. What have you lost?"
- **Garius:** "...Nothing. Forget it."
- **Saros** (to Garius) "You're sharp. Team Eclipse could use people like you."
- **Garius:** "Not interested."
- *Saros bows to the elder and leaves.*
- > Note: Hint G4: Garius brushes off the warning too hard, beside an Eclipse leader. In hindsight, "people like you" and "Not interested" are two people who already know each other.
- > Note: On the surface, Saros came to recruit followers at a famous shrine. In hindsight, he knew they'd be here: leak.
