# Arc 3: Garius's reveal to the League (draft)

Claude's draft, mirrored in the "Arc 3 Screenplay" tab of the story doc. Darren (the player, girl or boy) is a silent
protagonist apart from Yes/No choices. Lines marked Note, and bracketed asides, are for us, not in-game text.

## Scene 3.1: Hearthome City, the readings spike

- *The morning after Arc 2. The Hearthome Pokemon Center. Rowan, Ruth, Cyrus, Darren, and Garius, who's back, leaning on the wall.*
- **Ruth:** "Something huge just woke up at Lake Valor, southwest of here. The readings don't look like a rift. They look like... a clock. Ticking."
- **Rowan:** "The old palace at Valor was built for Dialga. If Eclipse has enough shards now..."
- **Cyrus:** "They have enough. That vein was enough."
- **Rowan:** "Then we go now. Through Route 212, the entrance at the top-left corner."
- **Garius** (pushing off the wall) "Right behind you."
- > Note: Garius hears the plan one last time. The player still has no reason to suspect him over anyone else, except in hindsight.

## Scene 3.2: Route 212, the reveal

- *The path into Lake Valor from the top-left corner of Route 212. Eclipse grunts block the way. Garius walks past Darren and stands with them, facing the group.*
- **Garius:** "Darren, let's not play any more games. It was me all along."
- **Ruth:** "...What?"
- **Garius:** "Ravaged Path. The Windworks. Celestic. Route 214. Route 213. The vein. All me."
- **Garius:** "You know what the best part was? I didn't even have to lie. You all wanted it to be him so bad."
- **Cyrus:** "...So it was you."
- **Garius:** "You made it easy. Thanks for that."
- **Rowan:** "Garius. Why?"
- **Garius:** "Because they're going to bring my dad back. That's why."
- **Garius:** "He died when I was little. Everybody tells me to let it go. Eclipse is the only one who said I didn't have to."
- *Rival battle: Garius.*
- **Garius** (after the battle) "Doesn't matter. You're already too late."
- *He runs into the palace grounds. The grunts close ranks behind him.*
- > Note: The informant reveal (S10). It's also the first time Garius says out loud that his father is dead (S5). Darren and the player finally hear what the hints were about.
- > Note: Fair-play check: every leak in Arc 2 is explainable by Garius, and the one raid he didn't know about (the Haven) is the one that worked.

## Scene 3.3: The clockwork palace, Dialga falls

- > Note: How you fail: Dialga stops time, and they walk out while you're frozen. You can't stop what you can't move through.

## Scene 3.17: After the battle

- *Garius's last Pokemon falls. Dialga and Palkia stop struggling in their chains. Garius drops to his knees beside the machine.*
- **Garius:** "..."
- **Garius:** "He used to say... he used to say Pokemon aren't tools. That you never hurt one to get what you want. Never."
- **Garius:** "He said it all the time. I forgot. How did I forget that?"
- **Garius:** "Everything I did... he'd have hated all of it."
- **Cyrus:** "I tried to remake a whole world because I couldn't bear the one I had. I hurt everything I touched to do it."
- **Cyrus:** "Your father knew better than both of us. It's not too late to be his son."
- *Garius reaches up and shuts the machine off. The shard chains shatter. Dialga and Palkia hang in the air for a moment, free, and look down at Darren. Then they're gone, and the sky closes.*
- **Garius:** "...I'm going home. I need to tell my mom."
- **Garius:** "No. I need to tell her everything."
- *He walks back down the waterfall path without looking back.*
- > Note: Garius's redemption (approved): he refuses the truth before the battle and accepts it only after he loses. Dialga and Palkia go free. Like the totems, they can be caught in the postgame.
