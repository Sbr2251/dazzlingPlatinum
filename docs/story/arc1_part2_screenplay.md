# Arc 1, part 2: home to Gym 1 (as built)

Mirrors scenes 7-14 of the "Act 1 Screenplay" tab of the story doc, which now matches the game on main @ 43f6445ca2 (2026-10-10). Scenes 1-6 (the drop-in at Lake Verity) are in the same tab. Round 2 and round 3 changes are folded in (`docs/arc1/round2/PLAN.md`, `docs/arc1/round3.md`).

Continues from the castle ending above. Darren (the player, who can be a girl or a boy; no in-game line assumes which) is a silent protagonist apart from Yes/No choices. Ruth works for Rowan as his assistant: a new character with a new sprite. Stage directions are in italics; highlighted notes are for us, not in-game text.

- **Garius:** "Hey, there's still one in there!"

- **Ruth:** "Professor, my readings at the portal spiked just like that, right before it closed."
- **Rowan:** "Then it came from that world. I'll need time with this."

- **Rowan:** "The man who guided you. Who is he?"

- **Saros** (on screen) "Everyone here has lost someone. A parent. A child. A partner who never came home."
- **Saros** (on screen) "My daughter would have turned nine this spring."
- **Saros** (on screen) "They tell you to accept it. To move on. Team Eclipse will not ask you to accept anything."
- **Saros** (on screen) "We are going to bring them back."
- **Text box:** "The crowd murmurs."
- *A grunt pushes a flyer into Garius's hands.*

- *The camera pans away from the kids to the east edge of the crowd and stops on a man in a trench coat. He glances east.*

- *Cyrus walks off into the city. The camera returns to the player, and Garius heads for Route 203.*

- *Mine B2F, north shaft: old crates wedged into a nook, a narrow crack behind them. "Squeeze through the crack?" The new Oreburgh Mine side tunnel: Roggenrola, Drilbur and Timburr (Lv 7-10) make up about 90% of encounters. At the end:*

## Scene 13c: Gym 1, the rock-slide puzzle

- *The Gym is a quarry. Two Machop winch crews each slide a rubble pile between two gates: the west winch moves it between the lower stairs and the east path, the east winch between the upper stairs and the lower stairs. You need both to reach Roark. Stock trainers Jonathon and Darius stay.*
- **Gym Guide:** "Roark's rigged this Gym like a real quarry. Rock chutes everywhere! Get a Machop to heave on a winch, and the rubble slides away... ...and piles up somewhere else! So watch where it lands."
- *Gym 1: Roark (Geodude 13, Onix 14, Cranidos 16; see the Trainer Changes tab). The player earns the Coal Badge and TM76 (Stealth Rock). All rubble is cleared after the badge.*

## Scene 14: After Gym 1, the rift in the mine (end of Arc 1)

- *Leaving the Gym: the screen shakes. A miner runs up.*
- **Miner:** "The sealed tunnel! It's glowing!"
- *Deep in the mine (B2F): where the sealed wall was, a small violet rift hangs in the air. Garius is already there. Rowan and Ruth come down the north shaft.*
- **Rowan:** "I came as soon as the readings spiked. ...So. Another one."
- *Through the rift, a huge silhouette rears up and roars: a Hitmonlee, far too big, wreathed in a violet aura. Screen shake, a white flash, and it's gone.*
- **Garius:** "Was that... a GIANT Hitmonlee?!"
- **Rowan:** "I've spent every hour since Lake Verity with that shard. It isn't a rock. It's energy. A Pokemon's pain, pressed solid."
- **Rowan:** "The Mawile you fought had a sliver of the same thing in it. That's why it attacked."
- **Rowan:** "A Pokemon caught on the far side of a rift like this one soaks that energy in until it swells. Huge. Furious. Suffering. ...A totem."
- **Ruth:** "Can't we pull the energy back out?"
- **Rowan:** "Not from this side. It's bound to that world. Defeat the totem there, in the Distortion World, and the energy should drain out of it. The Pokemon should go free."
- **Garius:** "So we go in and beat it! Easy!"
- **Rowan:** "You will do nothing of the kind without a guide. That world nearly swallowed you once."
- **Rowan:** "And I want to know why this rift is here at all. Rifts don't tear open on their own."
- *The rift shudders and shrinks to nothing.*
- **Ruth:** "Professor, it's collapsing! ...No, wait. The readings didn't drop. They moved. Something just opened up north of Jubilife, near Route 204."
- **Rowan:** "Then it isn't gone. It's waiting."
- *Roark has followed everyone down. It's his mine.*
- **Roark:** "My miners have been hearing that crying for a week. Every Pokemon in Oreburgh's been on edge since."
- **Roark:** "That badge you won means Oreburgh vouches for you. Calm that thing down, and I've got a feeling you'll find out what that's worth."

- **Rowan:** "There's one man who walks that world like he's been there before."
- **Garius:** "Spacesuit guy?"
- **Rowan:** "He was seen in Jubilife. Find him. Bring him to me."
- *Fade out. A card: END OF ARC 1. To be continued... The player can walk out normally; Jubilife's north exit shows "To be continued..."*
