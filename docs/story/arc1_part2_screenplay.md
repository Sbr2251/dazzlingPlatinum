# Arc 1, part 2: home to Gym 1 (draft)

Claude's draft, mirrored from the "Act 1 Screenplay" tab of the story doc. Scene 10 was updated to match the game
(2026-10-10). Story changes in progress are tracked in `docs/arc1/round2/PLAN.md`.

Continues from the castle ending above. Darren (the player, who can be a girl or a boy; no in-game line assumes which) is a silent protagonist apart from Yes/No choices. Ruth works for Rowan as his assistant: a new character with a new sprite. Stage directions are in italics; highlighted notes are for us, not in-game text.

## Scene 7: Coming home (Twinleaf Town)

- *Right after Act 1: Garius has run off home. The player walks back to Twinleaf.*
- *Player's house, 1F. Mom heals the party when you talk to her.*
- **Mom:** "There you are! The news said the portal closed, and I've been pacing ever since."
- **Mom:** "...Wait. You went INTO it? Didn't you?"
- **Mom:** "Well. You came back in one piece, with a Pokemon of your own. I suppose I can't be too angry. Let it rest a bit."
- *Optional: the TV in the living room.*
- **TV:** "...the portal above the Lake Verity castle has closed. Prof. Rowan declined to comment. In other news, Team Eclipse rallies continue to draw record crowds in Jubilife City..."
- *Optional: Garius's house. His mom is at the table. [Hint G1: his father]*
- **Garius's Mom:** "Oh, hello, dear. Garius ran in, shouted something about a portal, grabbed a sandwich, and ran right back out."
- **Garius's Mom:** "He's got his father's habit of running off before anyone can stop him."
- **Garius's Mom:** "...Keep an eye on him for me, would you?"
- > Note: The past tense ("his father's habit") is the whole hint. Nobody comments on it.

## Scene 8: Route 201, first battle with Garius

- *Garius waits at the Twinleaf edge of Route 201 and runs up when the player arrives.*
- **Garius:** "There you are! I've been waiting forever! ...Okay, like a minute."
- **Garius:** "Our Pokemon came out of the Distortion World, right? That makes them the toughest starters ever. So let's find out whose is tougher!"
- *Battle: Garius, with the starter strong against yours. Losing doesn't end the game, as in stock.*
- **Garius** (if you win) "Whoa... Okay, okay. Lucky break. Next time's mine!"
- **Garius** (if you lose) "Ha! Told you! Toughest starter ever!"
- **Garius:** "Rowan said to come by his lab in Sandgem, right? Last one there owes me 10 million!"
- *Garius runs off toward Sandgem.*

## Scene 9: Sandgem Town, Rowan's lab

- *Ruth is at the lab door.*
- **Ruth:** "Oh, it's you! The Professor's been expecting you. Well... his exact words were, "If those two reckless children show up, send them in.""
- *Inside: Rowan at his desk, Garius already there, bouncing on his heels.*
- **Rowan:** "Hm. You came. Good. Let me see your Pokemon."
- **Rowan:** "...Healthy. Strong, even. Whatever that world is, it didn't harm them."
- **Rowan:** "I mean to record every Pokemon in Sinnoh, and lately this region has been... changing. I can't keep up alone. Take these."
- *The player and Garius each get a Pokedex.*
- **Garius:** "Oh! Oh, show him the rock! The one the weird guy gave us!"
- *The player hands over the Eclipse Shard. Rowan holds it up to the light. (The shard leaves the bag; Rowan keeps it.)*
- **Rowan:** "...It's warm. No. It's pulsing. Like a heartbeat."
- **Ruth:** "Professor, my readings at the portal spiked just like that, right before it closed."
- **Rowan:** "Then it came from that world. I'll need time with this."
- **Rowan:** "The man who guided you. Who is he?"
- **Garius:** "No idea. Didn't even say his name. Talks like a fortune cookie. Dresses like he's going to space."
- **Rowan:** "He walked back into that world for two children he'd never met. Whoever he is... I'd like to thank him."
- > Note: Dramatic irony: the player knows exactly who he is. "Dresses like he's going to space" is a second Galactic joke only the player gets.
- **Rowan:** "Now go. Oreburgh City has a Gym, and you two have Pokemon. Isn't that what you wanted?"
- **Garius:** "Gym! Badges! Let's GO!"
- *Garius runs out.*
- **Ruth:** "Wait, do you even know how to catch a Pokemon? ...Come with me."

## Scene 10: Route 202, Ruth's gift

- *No catching tutorial: players are assumed to have played Platinum (owner, 2026-10-10 playtest). Ruth walks

## Scene 11: Jubilife City, the Eclipse rally

- *Entering the plaza in front of Jubilife TV: a crowd faces the big outdoor screen. Team Eclipse grunts hand out flyers under a banner: WHO DID YOU LOSE?*
- *Garius is at the front of the crowd. Walking up to him starts the scene.*
- *The screen shows a leader of Team Eclipse (Saros), speaking to the camera.*
- **Saros:** "Everyone here has lost someone. A parent. A child. A partner who never came home."
- **Saros:** "They tell you to accept it. To move on. Team Eclipse will not ask you to accept anything."
- **Saros:** "We are going to bring them back."
- *The crowd murmurs. A grunt pushes a flyer into Garius's hands.*
- **Eclipse Grunt:** "Everybody's lost somebody, kid. Think about it."
- *Garius stares at the flyer for a long moment. He folds it carefully and puts it in his pocket.*
- **Garius:** "...What? It's just a flyer. Come on, Oreburgh's this way."
- *Optional: a man in a trench coat at the edge of the crowd, scribbling in a notebook.*
- **Man in a Trench Coat:** "Hm. Hm hm. Don't mind me. I am simply a regular citizen. Who enjoys... rallies."
- > Note: Looker's first appearance. He's investigating Team Eclipse, and turns up again undercover at the Eclipse Haven in Eterna (Arc 2).
- *Camera pans away from the kids to the edge of the crowd: Cyrus, alone, with no Pokemon, watching the screen. Darren doesn't see him.*
- **Cyrus** (to himself) "Promising the impossible to people in pain... I know that voice. I used to have it."
- *Cyrus turns and walks off into the city. Camera returns to the player.*
- > Note: Three plants in one scene: Garius keeps the flyer (hint about his father, and a clue to the informant twist in hindsight); Cyrus's line (only the player hears it); Cyrus is in Jubilife, where he'll confess at the start of Arc 2.

## Scene 12: Route 203, second battle with Garius

- **Garius:** "Hey! I caught two more Pokemon already. Rematch! Right now!"
- *Battle: Garius.*
- **Garius:** "...Hey. That Eclipse guy on the screen. You think they could really do it? Bring people back?"
- *Yes/No choice.*
- **Garius** (Yes) "...Yeah. Yeah, maybe."
- **Garius** (No) "Yeah. ...Yeah, probably not."
- **Garius:** "Forget it. Race you to Oreburgh!"

## Scene 13: Oreburgh City and the mine

- *Stock Oreburgh: Roark is in the mine, then back at the Gym. Two new miner NPCs at the mine entrance:*
- **Miner:** "Something's wrong down in the deep shaft. We hear crying, like a Pokemon in pain. Real faint. Been going on for days. Boss sealed that tunnel off."
- **Miner:** "Saw some folks in dark coats hauling crates up by the old shaft one night. Figured they were surveyors."
- > Note: Plant for the player: dark coats, a crying Pokemon, a sealed tunnel. The characters don't connect them.
- *Gym 1: Roark (stock). The player earns the Coal Badge.*

## Scene 14: After Gym 1, the rift in the mine (end of Arc 1)

- *Leaving the Gym: the screen shakes. A miner runs up.*
- **Miner:** "The sealed tunnel! It's glowing!"
- *Deep in the mine: where the sealed wall was, a small violet rift hangs in the air. Garius is already there. Rowan and Ruth arrive.*
- **Rowan:** "I came as soon as the readings spiked. ...So. Another one."
- *Through the rift, a huge silhouette rears up and roars: a Hitmonlee, far too big, wreathed in a violet aura. Then it's gone.*
- *For one frame, a far larger shadow passes behind it. Nobody reacts. [Plant: Giratina watching. Only the player can notice.]*
- **Garius:** "Was that... a GIANT Hitmonlee?!"
- **Rowan:** "I've spent every hour since Lake Verity with that shard. It isn't a rock. It's energy. A Pokemon's pain, pressed solid."
- **Rowan:** "The Mawile you fought had a sliver of the same thing in it. That's why it attacked."
- **Rowan:** "A Pokemon caught on the far side of a rift like this one soaks that energy in until it swells. Huge. Furious. Suffering. ...A totem."
- **Ruth:** "Can't we pull the energy back out?"
- **Rowan:** "Not from this side. It's bound to that world. Defeat the totem there, in the Distortion World, and the energy should drain out of it. The Pokemon should go free."
- **Garius:** "So we go in and beat it! Easy!"
- **Rowan:** "You will do nothing of the kind without a guide. That world nearly swallowed you once."
- **Rowan:** "And I want to know why this rift is here at all. Rifts don't tear open on their own."
- *The rift shudders and shrinks to nothing.*
- **Ruth:** "Professor, it's collapsing! ...No, wait. The readings didn't drop. They moved. Something just opened up north of Jubilife, near Route 204."
- **Rowan:** "Then it isn't gone. It's waiting."
- *Roark has followed everyone down. It's his mine.*
- **Roark:** "My miners have been hearing that crying for a week. Every Pokemon in Oreburgh's been on edge since."
- **Roark:** "That badge you won means Oreburgh vouches for you. Calm that thing down, and I've got a feeling you'll find out what that's worth."
- > Note: Sets up the rule for every Gym: there are no HMs. Once you have a region's badge and have calmed its totem, you can channel that totem's energy to clear field obstacles. Ruth gives you the device for it after the first totem, early in Arc 2.
- **Rowan:** "There's one person who has walked that world and come back."
- **Garius:** "Spacesuit guy?"
- **Rowan:** "He was seen in Jubilife. Find him. Bring him to me."
- *Fade out. END OF ARC 1.*
- > Note: Hands off straight to Arc 2: the kids find Cyrus in Jubilife, and he gathers everyone to sit down and confess. The rift reopens at Ravaged Path on Route 204, where the existing Hitmonlee totem encounter already is, so that's the first totem fight in Arc 2, with Cyrus guiding.
