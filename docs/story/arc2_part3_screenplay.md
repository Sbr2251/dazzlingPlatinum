# Arc 2, part 3: Veilstone to the end of Arc 2 (draft)

Claude's draft, mirrored in the "Arc 2 Screenplay" tab of the story doc. Continues from part 2 (Celestic).

## Scene 2.17: Veilstone City, Gym 3, and Looker's check

- *Gym 3: Maylene. The player earns the Cobble Badge.*
- **Maylene:** "Ruth told me about the rift on Route 214. My students won't train down there anymore. They say the sky over it feels wrong. Please, help it."
- *Outside the Gym, a man in a hat and sunglasses is reading a newspaper upside down. He lowers it.*
- **Looker:** "Do not be alarmed. It is me. Looker. I am in disguise."
- **Looker:** "I ran the name you gave me. Cyrus. Every database in Sinnoh. The international records. Everything."
- **Looker:** "No birth record. No Trainer ID. No history. Nothing."
- **Looker:** "This man does not exist."
- **Looker:** "So I must ask. Do you trust him?"
- *Yes/No choice.*
- **Looker** (Yes) "Hm. Then I hope you are right. For your sake."
- **Looker** (No) "Hm. Then we understand each other. Keep your eyes open."
- > Note: Dramatic irony: the player knows exactly why there's no record. To the characters it looks sinister, and it lands right on top of the leaks.

## Scene 2.17b: The Meteor Shrine, Maylene's trial (Mega Evolution side plot)

- > Note: Dramatic irony: Cyrus's "only rocks" line. In the player's Platinum, the Veilstone meteorites really were just landmarks.

## Scene 2.21: Route 213, Looker's proof (leak L3)

- *Looker briefs the group at the Pastoria Pokemon Center: Darren, Cyrus, Ruth and Garius. A live Eclipse site, hidden in the caves on Route 213. They go in at nightfall.*
- **Looker:** "Tonight, we see it with our own eyes."
- *Nightfall, the cave. Two frames bolted to the wall. In one, a Lapras is already gone: there's only a rift, torn wide open and screaming. In the other, a Psyduck hangs limp, the air around it starting to split.*
- **Ruth:** "No, no, no... it's tearing! It's going to pull it through!"
- *Battle: Eclipse grunts guarding the machine. After the battle, Looker and Darren cut the Psyduck free just as the rift around it snaps shut.*
- *The other rift stays open. Something huge moves on the far side of it.*
- **Looker:** "They knew. They knew we were coming, so they rushed it. They pushed that machine to finish early."
- **Looker:** "That Lapras is in there because someone told them we were coming."
- *Silence. Nobody looks at anybody.*
- **Looker:** "Now I understand. They are not just taking Pokemon. They hurt them until the world itself tears. And whatever falls through comes out like... that."
- *[PUZZLE: the Route 213 rift. NEEDS DESIGN.] Totem battle: Lapras (the existing encounter). Calmed, it gives Darren its Calm Shard. [Field power: Surf, with the Fen Badge.]*
- > Note: Leak L3, the worst: the leak itself is why the Lapras became a totem. This is where the characters learn the truth about the totems (S4).

## Scene 2.23: Route 213 beach, the confrontation (third battle with Cyrus)

- *Night on the beach. Cyrus stands apart, looking at the water. Garius walks up beside Darren.*
- **Garius:** "Ravaged Path. The Windworks. Celestic. Route 214. Tonight. Every time, he's there. Every time, they know."
- **Garius:** "Looker can't find a single thing about him. Come on. You know it's him."
- **Garius:** "Make him prove it. Battle him."
- *Darren walks over to Cyrus. Cyrus turns before Darren can say anything.*
- **Cyrus:** "You don't need to say it. I've seen that look before. I used to wear it."
- **Cyrus:** "Words prove nothing. Judge me the way trainers do."
- *Battle 3 with Cyrus. He fights hard this time.*
- **Cyrus** (after the battle) "...Whatever you've decided, I won't stand here and wait to be believed."
- **Cyrus:** "Ruth's readings show a rift in the Lost Tower, near Hearthome. I'll go in alone. If there's proof of who's doing this, it's in that world. I'll find it."
- *Cyrus leaves. Ruth heads back to Sandgem with Looker's samples.*
- > Note: Battle 3 of 5 with Cyrus: the confrontation. Irony in hindsight: Garius, the informant, is the one who pushes Darren to fight him.

## Scene 2.26: The Lost Tower, the clearing leak, and finding Cyrus (end of Arc 2)

- *The next morning, at the Lost Tower rift: Eclipse grunts with mining picks and empty crates.*
- **Eclipse Grunt:** "Boss heard there's a whole vein down there. Grab everything."
- *Battle: the grunts. They flee.*
- *Darren stands at the rift. Cyrus has been inside for two days, with no way to reach anyone.*
- **Rowan** (arriving, out of breath) "I came as fast as I could. They knew about the vein? ...But only you, me and Garius knew."
- **Rowan:** "And Cyrus has been in there the whole time."
- **Rowan:** "...It wasn't him."
- *Darren goes back in. Now the wall of shadow parts. [Plant: Giratina letting Darren through.] Deeper, beside a towering Spiritomb, Cyrus is on his knees, exhausted, his starter fainted at his side. He's been trying to calm it alone.*
- **Cyrus:** "...You came."
- *Totem battle: Spiritomb (the existing encounter). Calmed, it gives Darren its Calm Shard. [Field power: clear fog, with the Relic Badge.]*
- *Back outside the tower. Rowan, and Garius, who has just arrived.*
- **Rowan:** "Cyrus. I doubted you. I'm sorry."
- **Cyrus:** "You were right to. I'd have doubted me too."
- *Garius looks at Cyrus for a long moment. His face gives nothing away.*
- **Garius:** "...Huh. Guess it wasn't him."
- *Garius turns and walks off toward Hearthome.*
- *Fade out. END OF ARC 2.*
- > Note: Cyrus is cleared: he was lost in the Distortion World when the vein leaked. Only one person who knew is left. Arc 3 opens with Garius's reveal.
- > Note: This is also when Eclipse learns the Distortion World holds shard veins. Harvesting them is what powers the Dialga and Palkia captures in Arc 3.
