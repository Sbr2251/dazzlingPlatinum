# Map changes tracker

Target: about 40% of the map is different from stock Platinum. Rule of thumb: every changed area should serve
the story or a catching goal.

Status: **done** (in the ROM), **planned** (decided), **candidate** (idea, not decided).

## Visible-change budget

Denominator: about 62 main-story places (14 towns, Routes 201-223, about 25 caves, lakes and buildings; no
postgame or Battle Zone). "Visible" means the layout or look changes, not just the events.

### Redesigns of dull areas (approved)

1. **Routes 216-217:** mountain pass with a memorial ridge (Teo's marker, scene 3.7) and Indra's camp. Arc 3.
2. **Solaceon Town:** the heart of Eclipse's public support: banners, a memorial wall of photos, NPCs who joined
   because they lost someone. Arc 2.
3. **Route 215:** flooded lowland with boardwalks; rain runoff from the Route 214 rift; Eclipse truck ruts to the
   Veilstone depot. Arc 2.
4. **Routes 206-207:** Eclipse's supply road into Mt. Coronet; staging yard at the Coronet south entrance; part of
   Cycling Road closed. Arc 3.
5. **Sandgem Town (stretch):** Rowan's lab gains a rift research annex (readings dish, Ruth's workshop). Arcs 1-3.
6. **Route 222 (stretch):** coast lit violet by the Route 223 rift; blacked-out lamps, stranded Water Pokemon. Arc 3.

Check: with the new gym order the player crosses north Route 210 (stock fog) right after Celestic, before the
fog-clearing power (Gym 5). Remove that fog or turn it into rift mist.
